Privacy Policy — Kharcha

Last updated: 2026

What data this app collects

Kharcha does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All expense entries, categories, notes, and budget amounts you enter are stored only on your own device, using your browser/app's local storage.

Data sharing

No data leaves your device. Kharcha has no server, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Data deletion

You can delete any entry directly in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage removes all saved data permanently.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the app developer through the Play Store listing.
